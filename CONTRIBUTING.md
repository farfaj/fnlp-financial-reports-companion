# Contributing

Thank you for helping improve this research companion.

## In scope

- Corrections that align a statement or aggregate with the published paper.
- Clearer descriptions of the paper's taxonomy, applications, limitations, or regional context.
- Updates to broken resource links, with an archived or authoritative replacement where possible.
- Accessibility, navigation, metadata, or citation improvements.

## Out of scope for version 1.x

- Python extraction code, model training, weights, notebooks, or deployment infrastructure.
- Claims of reproduction or benchmark performance that are not supported by a separately documented experiment.
- Uploads of the publisher PDF, eProofs, paywalled content, or third-party datasets and models without explicit redistribution permission.
- Study-level additions that would silently change the paper's original 231-study review corpus.

## Contribution checklist

1. Identify the page, section, table, or figure in the published paper that supports a paper-derived change.
2. Use paraphrase unless a short quotation is necessary; preserve citation and copyright boundaries.
3. Label any post-publication update as a companion update rather than a finding of the paper.
4. Keep CSV counts consistent with their documented totals.
5. Confirm that links use HTTPS where available.

By contributing, you agree that your contribution may be distributed under the repository's [CC BY 4.0 license](LICENSE.md) and that you have the right to submit it.
