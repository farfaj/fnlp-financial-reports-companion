# Reuse and rights guidance

## Repository material

You may reuse the repository's covered original content under CC BY 4.0. A suitable attribution is:

> Alarfaj, F. K. (2026). *Financial Report Extraction with NLP: Research Companion*, version 1.0.1. Companion to https://doi.org/10.1007/s13369-026-11157-6. Licensed CC BY 4.0.

Indicate what you changed and link to the license.

## Article material

The article and publisher-formatted materials are not covered by the repository license. Do not upload or redistribute the publisher PDF, eProof, figures, tables, or substantial article text through this repository unless the applicable publishing agreement and law explicitly permit it.

When sharing the research, link to the DOI. For self-archiving an accepted manuscript or another version, check the publishing agreement and the publisher's current policy.

## External resources

The paper discusses open-source, research, commercial, and proprietary resources. Before using any of them:

- read the resource's current license and terms;
- check whether weights, data, code, and documentation have different licenses;
- confirm redistribution and commercial-use rights;
- evaluate privacy, confidentiality, data residency, and API retention policies;
- preserve required notices and citations.

## License choice for a later code phase

CC BY 4.0 is well suited to documentation and original research summaries, but a later executable implementation should receive a software license chosen deliberately (for example, Apache-2.0 or MIT) after checking institutional policy, patent considerations, and third-party dependencies. Model weights and datasets may need separate licenses.

This guidance is informational and not legal advice.
